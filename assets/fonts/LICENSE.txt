Fonts used on this site
=======================

All files in this folder are subsets (Latin characters only) converted to WOFF
for use on the web. They are derived works of the fonts listed below.

inter-400.woff, inter-500.woff, inter-600.woff
  Inter 4.0 — Copyright 2016 The Inter Project Authors (https://github.com/rsms/inter)
  Licensed under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1 (https://openfontlicense.org).

display-700.woff  (internal name "CH Display")
  Derived from TeX Gyre Heros Cn Bold 2.004 — Copyright 2006, 2009 for TeX Gyre
  extensions by B. Jackowski and J. M. Nowacki (on behalf of TeX users groups).
  Original: https://www.gust.org.pl/projects/e-foundry/tex-gyre/heros

caption-400-italic.woff  (internal name "CH Caption")
  Derived from Latin Modern Roman 10 Italic 2.004 — Copyright 2003, 2009
  B. Jackowski and J. M. Nowacki (on behalf of TeX users groups).
  Original: https://www.gust.org.pl/projects/e-foundry/latin-modern

  The two fonts above are distributed under the GUST Font License (GFL), an
  instance of the LaTeX Project Public License 1.3c or later:
  https://www.gust.org.pl/projects/e-foundry/licenses/GUST-FONT-LICENSE.txt
  As the GFL requests, the derived files carry different font names.
