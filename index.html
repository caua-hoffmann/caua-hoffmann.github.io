About me

I’m Cauã, an electrical engineering student at the Federal University of Santa Catarina (UFSC), in Brazil. Since 2026 I have been in Toulouse for a dual degree at ENSEEIHT, Toulouse INP.

Electric machines have been my favorite subject since I started my degree. For three years I worked as an R&D research fellow at UFSC’s Vibration and Acoustics Laboratory, on projects with industry partners: simulating motors in Ansys Maxwell, automating a test bench, and measuring noise, vibration and efficiency.

My work combines numerical modeling, scientific programming and experimental validation in the lab.

R&D experience

Three years at the Vibration and Acoustics Laboratory (LVA) of UFSC, on research projects with industry partners. The projects are confidential, so the descriptions below stay general.

January 2025 – August 2026

R&D research fellow

Project with Nidec
Vibration and Acoustics Laboratory (LVA), UFSC

  • Programmed the PLC (Ladder) and the HMI of a compact, semi-automated test bench that brings compressors to specific operating conditions.
  • Ran 2D and 3D electromagnetic simulations in Ansys Maxwell, integrated with Ansys Workbench.
  • Carried out motor efficiency and performance tests in cooperation with GRUCAD, UFSC’s electromagnetic devices research group.

January 2024 – December 2024

R&D research fellow

Project with Nidec
Vibration and Acoustics Laboratory (LVA), UFSC

  • Assessed noise and vibration in permanent-magnet synchronous motors (PMSM).
  • Ran acoustic and vibration tests in a semi-anechoic chamber with the Siemens LMS Test.Lab system (SCADAS).
  • Processed and analyzed measurement and simulation data in Python and MATLAB.
  • Simulated radial forces and torque in Ansys Maxwell.

August 2023 – December 2023

R&D research fellow

Project with Electrolux
Vibration and Acoustics Laboratory (LVA), UFSC

  • Analyzed the acoustic performance of refrigeration systems and ways to mitigate their noise sources.
  • Took measurements in reverberation and semi-anechoic chambers, using Brüel & Kjær’s B&K Connect software.
  • Processed the data in Python and MATLAB and wrote technical reports for the partner’s engineering team.

The acquisition system behind the measurements

Throughout my time at the laboratory I used SCADAS acquisition systems to record electrical, acoustic, vibration and thermal data from many different systems.

Projects

Electrical design of a residential building

Tools AutoCAD, MS Excel

Chorus effect pedal

An audio effect pedal for guitar, from simulation to the assembled unit: a PT2399 delay line, a low-frequency oscillator built around a TL072, an anti-latch-up circuit, LM78L05 voltage regulation and 3PDT true-bypass switching.

I can’t play the guitar. I built this pedal because I have always been fascinated by how much filters and electronic components shape the world of music.
